## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use the Service and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

Sanatan Kosh (सनातन कोश) is an offline Hindi reading app for Hindu aarti, chalisa, stotra, vrat and scriptures. The Application is designed to work without an internet connection after it is installed. We do not operate accounts, ads, analytics, or our own servers that receive Your Personal Data from the Application.

## Information We Collect

We do not require You to create an account, and We do not ask You to provide a name, email address, phone number, or other contact details inside the Application.

The Application does not include advertising, crash reporting, or analytics SDKs, and it is not designed to send Your reading activity to Our servers.

### Data stored only on Your Device

To provide reading features, the Application stores preferences and reading state locally on Your Device. This may include:

- Bookmarks (saved document identifiers)
- Last-read document and scroll position, so You can continue reading
- Display settings such as font size, dark theme, and whether verse meaning (artha) is shown

This information stays on Your Device unless You use a Device backup feature that copies app data (see below). It is not used to identify You to Us.

### Voice search

The Application may offer a microphone button so You can speak a search term (for example, a deity or granth name). If You use this feature, the Application opens the Device's system speech recognition service (often provided by Google or another keyboard/assistant app installed on Your Device). We receive only the transcribed text that that service returns, and We use it to fill the search box. We do not record, store, or upload the audio ourselves.

The speech recognition provider processes Your voice according to its own privacy policy. You can choose not to use voice search and type instead.

### Device backup

Android may back up Application data (such as bookmarks and settings) to Your Google Account if You have Android Backup enabled on the Device. That backup is controlled by Google and Your Device settings, not by a server We operate. You can turn off backup or clear app data in Android settings.

### Information You send by email

If You contact Us at the email address in this Privacy Policy, We will receive whatever You include in that message (for example, Your email address and the content of Your question) so We can reply.

### Information We do not collect

We do not collect:

- Names, phone numbers, or payment information inside the Application
- Location data
- Contacts, photos, files, or other media from Your Device
- Advertising IDs or usage analytics
- Cookies or similar tracking technologies in the Application

## How Information Is Used

Local Application data is used only to provide and maintain the Service on Your Device, including:

- **To provide and maintain Our Service:** remember bookmarks, last-read position, and display settings.
- **To run search:** use text You type, or transcribed text from voice search, to find content already stored in the Application.
- **To manage Your requests:** if You email Us, to read and respond to Your message.

We do not use Application data for advertising, profiling, or selling data.

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell Your Personal Data.

We do not share local reading data from the Application with third parties, because that data is stored on Your Device and is not sent to Us.

Information may be processed by others only in these limited cases:

- **Voice search:** if You use the microphone, the Device's speech recognition provider processes Your spoken audio as described above.
- **Device backup:** if Android Backup is enabled, Google (or Your Device manufacturer, as applicable) may store a backup of Application preferences in Your account.
- **App stores:** Google Play and similar stores may collect install, update, and store-listing interaction data under their own policies. We do not control that collection.
- **With Your consent:** We may disclose information You send Us if You ask Us to do so.

## Retention of Data

Local Application data remains on Your Device until You change it in the Application, clear the Application's storage in Device settings, or uninstall the Application.

Email You send Us is retained only as long as needed to respond to Your request and for any related follow-up, and then deleted or stored only as required to comply with law, resolve disputes, or keep a record of correspondence.

We do not operate a user-account database for the Application, so there is no cloud copy of bookmarks or reading progress held by Us.

## Delete Your Data

You can remove local Application data at any time by:

- Clearing bookmarks and changing settings inside the Application
- Using Android Settings → Apps → Sanatan Kosh → Storage → Clear data (wording may vary by Device)
- Uninstalling the Application

If You used voice search, any audio processed by the Device's speech recognition provider is governed by that provider's tools and policy, not by in-app deletion.

To request deletion of email correspondence You sent Us, contact Us at the address below. We may retain information when We have a legal obligation or lawful basis to do so.

## Security

Because reading data stays on Your Device, its security depends largely on Your Device lock screen, Android updates, and who can access the Device. Email You send Us is protected with commercially reasonable care, but no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's Privacy

The Service is a general-audience reading library. We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from anyone under the age of 16, and the Application does not ask children for Personal Information.

If You are a parent or guardian and You believe Your child has provided Us with Personal Information (for example, by emailing Us), please contact Us. If We become aware that We have collected Personal Information from anyone under the age of 16, We will take steps to delete it as soon as reasonably possible.
